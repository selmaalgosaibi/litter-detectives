Before you go out

  • One phone or tablet per team. Open this page once while you have signal; logging works without it.
  • Glass, syringes and sharp metal are logged from sight. Only staff pick them up.
  • Log items larger than 2.5 cm. Smaller pieces go in the microplastics sample.
  • Driftwood and shells are natural, so don't log them. Processed wood counts.
  • If you can't tell what an item is, log it as Other and describe it in Notes.
